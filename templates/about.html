About HealthAI Care

Welcome to HealthAI Care, where modern machine learning meets healthcare accessibility for a healthier, more informed future.

Our Vision

We envision a world where reliable healthcare knowledge is readily accessible to everyone. Navigating early symptoms can be overwhelming; our platform empowers individuals with intelligent data-driven insights to make informed decisions before consulting a doctor.

How It Works

Our core architecture is built upon a Support Vector Classifier (SVC) trained on extensive medical symptoms-disease correlation datasets. Users input their observed symptoms, and the model analyzes multi-dimensional symptom vectors to identify the most probable condition, paired with recommended precautions, medications, workouts, and diets.

Patient-Centric Guidance

We provide holistic recommendations: not just predicting a condition, but outlining practical, non-clinical steps including hygiene precautions, common OTC medications, dietary adjustments, and active physical recovery routines.

Educational Purpose: This application is built as an engineering demonstration of Applied Machine Learning in Healthcare and is not an autonomous diagnostic device.
